Our Story

Welcome to Hiramani

Nestled on NH-52 in the historic town of Fatehpur Shekhawati, Hiramani Hotel & Restaurant offers travelers a warm retreat in the heart of Rajasthan's painted haveli country.

Whether you're a business traveler, a tourist exploring the UNESCO-recognized frescoed havelis, or a family looking for a comfortable overnight stay — we provide clean, air-conditioned rooms with honest hospitality and home-style Rajasthani food.

Our philosophy is simple: high comfort at low prices, with service that makes you feel at home.
